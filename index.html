SA-EffNetB3 — Retinal Grading Model

A five-class diabetic-retinopathy classifier built on an ImageNet-pretrained EfficientNetB3 backbone. A custom spatial-attention block learns where to focus before the model assigns a severity grade.

Research and education only—not a diagnosis. The hosted app processes uploads on its server; do not upload real patient images or identifiers.
